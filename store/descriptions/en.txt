debait helps you understand what is behind a headline before spending time on the article. It uses AI to suggest a plain title, summarize key points, and assess clickbait framing with a 0–100 score.

HOW TO USE
• Analyze the current article from the toolbar button or right-click menu.
• Preview a link using the hover mode selected in Settings: Shift, Alt, or hovering without a modifier.
• Right-click a link and choose “debait this link” to preview it without opening it.

Shift previews require pointer movement; pressing Shift alone does not start a preview. Hovering without a modifier is optional and may fetch linked pages, transmit their content, and consume AI quota without another click.

SETUP AND CONTROL
Use your own AI-provider API key or a configured local model. Provider charges may apply. Settings includes a connection test, output-language selection, custom instructions, and preview usage limits. Save applies changes; Discard restores this Settings page’s last saved settings. Connection testing does not save settings.

LIMITATIONS
Analysis uses up to 12,000 characters of extracted text. Dynamic or paywalled pages may be unreadable. AI scores and summaries are not fact-checks or guarantees of accuracy. Preview limits do not cover manual current-page analysis or connection tests. Cancellation cannot undo requests already sent or their costs; a reserved preview attempt may still count.

PRIVACY
Selected page URLs, titles, meta descriptions, extracted text, and custom instructions go to your configured AI endpoint—not a developer-operated analysis server. Keys and preferences may synchronize through your browser’s sync service.

Custom / OpenAI-compatible endpoints require an explicit API base URL and model. Keys are optional for keyless services. Keyless remote HTTP sends content unencrypted; use an endpoint operator you trust. Keyed custom endpoints require HTTPS except for loopback addresses.
