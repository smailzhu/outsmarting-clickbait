Hover preview limits

Preview fetches + summarizes a link before you click. These caps stop bursty hovering from blowing your provider quota.

Hold Shift and move over a link; pressing Shift alone will not preview. Hover, do not click: Shift+click can open a new window. Alt release can expose browser menus. Hover without a modifier contacts linked sites and sends page text to your AI endpoint, using quota and potentially costing money, even at 0 ms dwell. Cancellation cannot refund already contacted sites or sent AI work. This setting shares your sync profile and applies only after Save.

Discard restores your last saved settings on this page (not defaults).
